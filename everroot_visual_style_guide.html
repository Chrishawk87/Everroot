Visual Identity  ·  The Living Legacy Forest

EverRootVisual Style Guide

The most emotionally beautiful digital place ever made — a sacred valley where families return for generations. Everything here must communicate warmth, peace, wisdom, hope, and timelessness. Golden hour, grounded photorealism, one colossal tree that is a human life.

The Master Tree · Hero Language

The anchor of the identity. A single 300-year-old sacred tree — 25 ft trunk, a canopy spanning hundreds of feet — read across four canonical camera positions. All four are approved as the visual north star.

The World · Valley & Community

The tree lives inside a whole living valley: wooden paths, streams and waterfalls, bridges, ancient stones, mist and god rays. It should feel like a place, not a backdrop.

Materials · Surface Reference

Grounded, scanned-quality surface studies — the texture bible for production assets. Even lighting, macro detail, no fantasy. The bark alone carries the only permitted "magic": a barely-perceptible golden vein in one crack.

Color Palette

Warm earth tones, deep greens, golden light, muted blue distance. Nothing oversaturated — a premium, luxury restraint.

Craft Rules

Lighting

  • Golden hour is the default. Warm, low, directional sun.
  • Volumetric god rays & morning mist; global illumination.
  • Subsurface scattering on every leaf; soft shadows; ambient occlusion.
  • Bloom kept subtle; cinematic exposure; true HDR.

Camera & Lens

  • 35mm — vistas, establishing scale.
  • 50mm — natural hero & environment.
  • 85mm — intimate, shallow depth, tenderness.
  • Movement is human, never robotic. Cinematic easing.

Composition

  • Rule of thirds; leading lines; natural framing.
  • Distinct foreground / midground / background.
  • Atmospheric perspective; negative space; depth.

Mood

  • Grounded photorealism — pull back overt "magic."
  • Peace over spectacle; the viewer should stop and stare.
  • Nothing static — everything gently breathes and moves.

Do / Don't

Always

  • Golden hour, warm + grounded, photoreal.
  • One dominant sacred tree; sense of scale.
  • Layered depth, mist, restrained palette.
  • Deliver finals upscaled to 4K.

Never

  • Oversaturation, neon, heavy bloom.
  • Low-poly, placeholder, or "programmer art."
  • Overt fantasy glow (memory vein stays subtle).
  • Flat, static, or robotic compositions.
