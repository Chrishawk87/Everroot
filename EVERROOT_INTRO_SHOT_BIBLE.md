# EVERROOT — "SAY SOMETHING" · SHOT BIBLE
### The complete director's breakdown — lenses, angles, blocking, wardrobe, performance, motion, sound

**Runtime:** ~1:52 · **18 shots** · **Aspect:** 2.39:1 anamorphic hero (9:16 reframe map at end)
**Camera package (or its AI equivalent):** full-frame sensor look, anamorphic bokeh, 35mm film grain overlay, subtle gate weave.

---

## 1 · THE VISUAL SYSTEM (read this first — it governs every shot)

**Two registers, one light.**
- **Register A — INTIMATE DOC (Beats 0–4, 6):** real people, real rooms. Handheld with a *breathing* stillness — never shaky, never locked. Available-light look. This is where the grief lives.
- **Register B — EVERROOT FOREST (Beat 5 + tree moments):** the luminous legacy tree. Smooth, motorized, reverent moves — dolly/crane/drone. This is where the hope lives.

The cut between them (Shot 12→13) is the emotional hinge of the whole film. A had *closes in*; B *opens out*.

**Lens philosophy.**
- **85mm** = grief and confession. Compresses, isolates, throws the world into a soft wash so the face is the only thing that exists. Loss is claustrophobic — the long lens agrees.
- **50mm** = truth/memory. Sees like the human eye. Used for the "ordinary life" and the "turn" — moments that should feel remembered, not staged.
- **35mm** = togetherness and world. Lets a room, a couple, a canopy breathe. Used when we widen from a person to their context.
- **24mm** = awe. Only once — the epic pull-back over the forest (S15). Save it, so it lands.

**Camera-height grammar.** Grief is shot at or slightly *below* eye line (the subject has quiet dignity, never pitied from above). The tree is shot from *low* looking up (we are small before it). The child (S16) is shot at *her* height (we kneel to wonder).

**Grade.** One unbroken golden-dusk LUT across both registers: warm amber highlights, deep green-black shadows, muted desaturated sky, skin kept warm and true. Grief and remembrance are the same room at a different hour — never let the sad shots go cold-blue and the happy shots go warm; keep them *one world*. The only shift is **density of light**: Beat 2–3 is underlit and negative-space heavy; Beat 5 onward the frame fills with light.

**Wardrobe philosophy.** Muted, lived-in earth tones — oatmeal, ochre, faded indigo, olive, rust, cream. Nothing branded, nothing that reads "styled." Natural fibers with wear. The rule: *no one should look like an actor.* Costume must disappear so the face carries everything. Only in Beat 5/6 does warm gold enter the wardrobe, catching the tree-light.

**The relay (edit logic).** No character owns a full sentence. The voiceover baton passes across faces and borders so a dozen strangers become one grieving, hoping human voice. Cut on breath and on gesture, not on line-ends.

## 2 · THE CAST (physicality · wardrobe · what they carry)

- **ODESSA — 78, Black American, New Orleans.** Broad, steady hands; reading glasses on a beaded chain; silver hair pinned back. *Wardrobe:* faded floral housedress, cream apron dusted with flour, worn gold wedding band she still wears. *Carries:* a recipe no one wrote down.
- **TOM — 71, white, rural Yorkshire.** Tall, stooped, big knuckled hands. *Wardrobe:* moss-green wool cardigan over a soft collared shirt, corduroy. *Carries:* a table set for two out of habit.
- **DANIEL — 34, Korean American, Los Angeles.** Lean, tired eyes, day-old stubble. *Wardrobe:* charcoal crewneck, simple. Modern, urban. *Carries:* a voicemail he's afraid to lose.
- **AMARA — 29, Nigerian, Lagos.** Warm, expressive, visibly pregnant. *Wardrobe:* deep ochre wrap dress, gold hoop earrings, a single beaded bracelet from her father. *Carries:* a child who will never meet its grandfather.
- **CAMILA — 11, Mexican, Oaxaca.** Serious dark eyes, hair in a loose braid. *Wardrobe:* simple embroidered blouse, faded, hand-me-down. *Carries:* her abuelo's stories, and the fear she's already losing his voice.
- **YUSUF — 62, Lebanese, Beirut.** Kind, grey-bearded, unhurried. *Wardrobe:* linen shirt rolled at the sleeve, worn leather watch. *Carries:* proverbs for grandchildren who live an ocean away.
- **PRIYA — 40, Indian, Mumbai.** Composed, holding it together. *Wardrobe:* muted teal kurta, small gold studs. *Carries:* a phone she can't bring herself to delete.
- **NILAK — 8, Inuit, Nunavut.** Round-cheeked, curious. *Wardrobe:* hand-knit sweater, warm layers. *Carries:* a song being taught to her through a screen. *(Full-film only — Beat 1/3 texture.)*

### BEAT 0 — BLACK (the fear, before the film) · 0:00–0:07

**S01 ⭐ — The Record Light**
- **Framing:** extreme CU, single point of red light in a sea of black; rule-of-thirds low-right so the void dominates.
- **Lens:** 85mm macro. **Angle:** dead-on, eye-level. **Movement:** near-imperceptible push-in (a held breath).
- **Light:** the red LED is the *only* source; one soft fill catching dust motes.
- **Who:** none — a disembodied voice (Yusuf, O.S.).
- **Emotional point:** we open on the universal impulse — *"Say something. Anything."* Before you know what EverRoot is, you feel the instinct to capture a voice.
- **Animation:** dust drifts through the glow; the dot pulses once, softly.
- **Sound:** room-tone hiss, one gentle breath. A single piano note on the pulse.
- **Cut:** the super *"The first thing you forget…"* fades in, then hard to light and life.

---

### BEAT 1 — THE ORDINARY LIGHT (un-filmed life) · 0:07–0:28
*Handheld, warm, imperfect — the footage nobody thought to keep. Golden hour through every window.*

**S02 — Odessa's Kitchen**
- **Framing:** MCU, she's frame-left, steam and window light frame-right (negative space that S06 will later fill with absence).
- **Lens:** 50mm. **Angle:** eye-level, slight over-shoulder into the pot. **Movement:** gentle handheld float, drifting with her sway.
- **Light:** hard golden backlight through a lace curtain; warm bounce on her face.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Odessa, housedress + flour-dusted apron.
- **Emotional point:** joy that is *unrecorded.* She hums, no words — the sound we'll ache for later.
- **Animation:** steam rises; spoon folds roux; shoulders sway to the hum.
- **Sound:** wooden spoon on cast iron, humming. Cello enters under.
- **Cut:** match-on-warmth to Tom's tea steam.

**S03 — Tom & Her (the two of them)**
- **Framing:** loose 2-shot, both slightly off-center; the WOMAN kept in soft focus / partial frame (we never quite see her — she is already becoming memory).
- **Lens:** 35mm. **Angle:** eye-level, across the table. **Movement:** slow handheld drift-in.
- **Light:** low afternoon sun raking across the table; two mugs catch a highlight (planted for S05).
- **Who/wardrobe:** Tom, green cardigan; Her, cream, soft-focus.
- **Emotional point:** ordinary devotion. Laughter at a joke we don't hear — intimacy we're eavesdropping on.
- **Animation:** both laughing, tea steaming, curtain breathing.
- **Sound:** muffled laughter, a spoon, a clock. VO relay begins across S02–S04.
- **Cut:** on the shared laugh.

**S04 — Amara Dancing With Her Father**
- **Framing:** MS, the two turning through frame, string-light bokeh behind.
- **Lens:** 50mm. **Angle:** eye-level, slightly low. **Movement:** handheld follow, a half-spin whip of motion blur.
- **Light:** warm practical string lights; magic-hour ambient.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Amara in ochre (not yet pregnant here — this is *before*); Father in a linen shirt.
- **Emotional point:** peak aliveness — the joy at its absolute fullest, so the fall in Beat 2 has height to fall from.
- **Animation:** a spin, heads back in laughter, lights sparkle and streak.
- **Sound:** distant music, laughter swells — then a beat of near-silence to pivot.
- **Cut:** hard cut to stillness. Music drops to a single sustained note.

### BEAT 2 — THE ABSENCE (the same rooms, emptier) · 0:28–0:50
*Identical setups to Beat 1 — same lenses, same blocking — but the person is gone and the light has thinned.*

**S05 ⭐ — The Second Mug**
- **Framing:** CU insert on the tabletop; two mugs, then one hand withdrawing the second; empty chair blurred deep.
- **Lens:** 85mm macro. **Angle:** low, table-level (we're small in the loss). **Movement:** locked, one breath of drift; holds on the empty ring of tea.
- **Light:** the golden rake of S03 now flat and cool-neutral; the highlight gone.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Tom's hand only (green cardigan cuff).
- **Emotional point:** THE quietest gut-punch. A habit meeting a fact. The scrape of the mug is the loudest sound in the film.
- **Animation:** hand enters, sets the mug, holds, withdraws; hold on the empty space 1.5s.
- **Sound:** room-tone only + one wooden scrape. No music.
- **Cut:** slow — let the empty space breathe before we leave it.

**S06 — Odessa, Alone at the Stove**
- **Framing:** same MCU as S02, but the warm window negative-space is now dim and unfilled.
- **Lens:** 50mm. **Angle:** eye-level. **Movement:** static handheld, barely alive.
- **Light:** the golden backlight is gone; a single flat overhead. Skin still warm but unlit by joy.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Odessa, same apron — a costume echo that hurts.
- **Emotional point:** *what did Mama put in it?* Memory failing in real time.
- **Animation:** lifts the spoon, hesitates, sets it down, looks at nothing.
- **Sound:** no humming now. Just the pot. The silence where a voice should be.
- **Cut:** to Daniel — night, colder.

**S07 ⭐ — Daniel & the Phone**
- **Framing:** CU, face frame-right, darkness frame-left.
- **Lens:** 85mm. **Angle:** eye-level, dead intimate. **Movement:** motionless, the faintest breath of push.
- **Light:** single-source — the phone screen, cold and low, underlighting; everything else falls to black.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Daniel, charcoal crewneck.
- **Emotional point:** the modern grief — a number that used to be a person. Thumb hovering over a name.
- **Animation:** screen light flickers; eyes glisten; a slow blink; eyes close.
- **Sound:** his line — *"I called just to hear the voicemail… the number was reassigned. A stranger answered."* Silence after. Cello holds an aching chord.
- **Cut:** to Camila — the youngest voice, so it lands hardest.

*(Full film adds: the pregnant-Amara doorway shot, S+ )*

### BEAT 3 — THE CONFESSION (faces, direct address) · 0:50–1:05
*Everyone admits the same private fear, as one voice. Same lens for all — 85mm — so the faces feel like one continuous confession.*

**S08 — Priya**
- **Framing:** CU, centered, symmetrical (a confession looks you in the eye).
- **Lens:** 85mm. **Angle:** eye-level, lens on her eyeline (she talks to *us*). **Movement:** locked.
- **Light:** soft warm key, gentle falloff; a catchlight kept alive in the eye.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Priya, teal kurta, phone held to her chest.
- **Emotional point:** *"I keep the voicemail because—"* — the reason we're all afraid.
- **Animation:** a slow blink, a held breath, a faint nod.
- **Sound:** her line, then handed off mid-sentence.
- **Cut:** match-cut on the same framing to Camila — different face, same sentence.

**S09 — Camila**
- **Framing:** identical CU to S08 (the match is the point).
- **Lens:** 85mm. **Angle:** at *her* eye-level (we lower to a child). **Movement:** locked.
- **Light:** warm dusk from a window; softer, younger.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Camila, embroidered blouse.
- **Emotional point:** *"And now there's no one left to ask."* A child naming the thing adults can't.
- **Animation:** eyes well; she holds; one blink lets it nearly fall — doesn't.
- **Sound:** the sentence completes. Cello finds a second voice.
- **Cut:** to the turn — a hand reaching for a phone.

### BEAT 4 — THE TURN (record instead of mourn) · 1:05–1:22
*The hinge of hope. Register A warms back up — golden light returns, camera comes gently alive.*

**S10 ⭐ — Yusuf Records**
- **Framing:** MS, him at the table, phone propped upright in the foreground (soft), him sharp behind.
- **Lens:** 50mm. **Angle:** eye-level, slight over-the-phone. **Movement:** slow, hopeful push-in.
- **Light:** warm practical lamp restored; golden key on his face — the first warmth since Beat 1.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Yusuf, rolled-sleeve linen, leather watch.
- **Emotional point:** *"This is for you. For the one I might not get to meet."* The fear turned into an act.
- **Animation:** he reaches, taps record, settles, a soft hopeful smile grows.
- **Sound:** his line, warm and unhurried. Piano returns, major key.
- **Cut:** to Amara — the same gesture, higher stakes.

**S11 — Amara Records to Her Belly**
- **Framing:** MCU, phone resting on her pregnant belly, hand cradling it.
- **Lens:** 50mm. **Angle:** eye-level, slightly low (reverent). **Movement:** slow float in.
- **Light:** golden window key; a halo rim on her hair.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Amara, ochre wrap, father's beaded bracelet visible on the cradling wrist.
- **Emotional point:** *"Hi, baby. This is your grandfather laughing. I want you to know exactly what he sounded like."* Loss and new life in one frame.
- **Animation:** an inhale; a smile breaks through tears.
- **Sound:** her line cracks and recovers. Strings lift.
- **Cut:** to the record dot — callback.

**S12 ⭐(as S12/close hybrid) — The Dot Blooms**
- **Framing:** CU, the S01 red dot — now swelling and blooming from red into warm gold.
- **Lens:** 85mm macro. **Angle:** dead-on. **Movement:** slow push as the bloom spreads to fill frame.
- **Light:** the dot becomes the light source, gold spilling outward into the black.
- **Emotional point:** the exact instant of transformation — capture becomes keeping. Fear becomes legacy.
- **Animation:** red→gold bloom, light spreading, dust igniting.
- **Sound:** super *"So keep it. All of it."* A single heartbeat drum enters.
- **Cut:** the gold spill *becomes* the rising motes of the forest — Register A → B.

### BEAT 5 — EVERROOT IGNITES (the product, finally) · 1:22–1:40
*Register B. Motorized, reverent, awe. We were never allowed to see the tree until now.*

**S13 ⭐ — The Tree Ignites**
- **Framing:** WIDE, the colossal tree centered, trunk rising out of low mist; canopy dark above.
- **Lens:** 35mm. **Angle:** LOW, looking up (we are small before it). **Movement:** slow crane-up + push, following the light as it rises.
- **Light:** golden memory-veins ignite up the bark; points of light rise into the canopy; volumetric god-rays.
- **Emotional point:** relief and awe — the answer to the fear made visible. Every recorded voice is a light going home.
- **Animation:** motes rise from below; veins ignite upward like current; mist drifts; rays sweep.
- **Sound:** VO — *"EverRoot. Every voice you love — rooted, and growing."* Choir opens; heartbeat drum steadies.
- **Cut:** to the branches — detail.

**S14 — The Lanterns Kindle**
- **Framing:** MEDIUM, a bough across frame, lanterns strung along it.
- **Lens:** 50mm. **Angle:** eye-level with the branch (we're up in it now). **Movement:** slow lateral dolly.
- **Light:** each lantern kindles in sequence — warm pools blooming down the limb; fireflies.
- **Emotional point:** a life has *parts* — advice, recipes, traditions, stories, messages for the future. Each lantern is a category of a person.
- **Animation:** lanterns light one-by-one in a run; fireflies drift; gentle sway.
- **Sound:** soft chimes as each lights; choir sustains.
- **Cut:** pull wider.

**S15 — The Forest of the Remembered**
- **Framing:** EPIC WIDE aerial, a whole valley of glowing trees, mist between them.
- **Lens:** 24mm (the film's only true wide — earn it). **Angle:** high, drifting aerial. **Movement:** slow drone pull-back revealing more and more trees.
- **Light:** each tree glows with its own warm memory-light; god-rays between; muted dusk sky.
- **Emotional point:** you are not alone in this. Everyone loses; everyone can keep. A community of the remembered.
- **Animation:** aerial pull-back, mist rolling, distant lights twinkling in.
- **Sound:** choir at its fullest, then begins to settle.
- **Cut:** down and in — to a single small hand.

---

### BEAT 6 — REUNION ACROSS TIME (the payoff) · 1:40–1:48

**S16 ⭐ — The Child & the Lantern**
- **Framing:** MCU, low, at her height; the lantern glowing just above her reach.
- **Lens:** 50mm. **Angle:** her eye-level (we kneel into wonder). **Movement:** a gentle push as she touches it.
- **Light:** the lantern's warm glow is her key light, dancing gold on her face; fireflies.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Amara's child, a few years old — a wisp of gold in the wardrobe now (Register B).
- **Emotional point:** the whole film pays off here — a child laughing at a joke from a grandfather who died before she was born. *His voice, kept.*
- **Animation:** she reaches, touches; the lantern pulses warm; she laughs upward.
- **Sound:** from the lantern, warm and whole — the Father's wedding laugh from S04. *"There she is. There's my girl."*
- **Cut:** to Daniel — resolution of his grief.

**S17 — Daniel at Peace**
- **Framing:** CU (rhyme with S07 — same man, same lens, transformed light).
- **Lens:** 85mm. **Angle:** eye-level. **Movement:** the faintest settle.
- **Light:** where S07 was cold phone-underlight, this is warm gold washing over him — the tree-light, or memory itself.
- **Who/wardrobe:** Daniel, charcoal crewneck (the S07 echo).
- **Emotional point:** grief resolved into presence. No stranger this time — her actual voice, and peace.
- **Animation:** face softens; a slow exhale; the smallest smile; a single tear, but at rest.
- **Sound:** his mother's voice, low and warm (unintelligible-tender). Score thins toward the close.
- **Cut:** to black-and-gold.

---

### BEAT 7 — CLOSE · 1:48–1:52

**S18 ⭐ — Back to the Light**
- **Framing:** CU, everything settled to the single gold point in soft darkness (the S01/S12 dot, now gold and calm).
- **Lens:** 85mm macro. **Angle:** dead-on. **Movement:** still; the world darkens around the point, which holds.
- **Light:** one warm point; dust motes at rest.
- **Emotional point:** the fear we opened on is answered. The light doesn't go out.
- **Animation:** everything darkens except the gold point, which stays lit.
- **Sound:** half a second of a real recorded laugh, then quiet. The REC dot from the top of the film would go dark — but the light in the tree stays.
- **End card:** **EVERROOT** · *The people we love don't have to fade.*

## 4 · EMOTIONAL ARCHITECTURE (the curve)

```
JOY      S02 S03 S04
              \
ABSENCE        S05 S06 S07
                        \
FEAR                     S08 S09   ← lowest point (a child names it)
                              /
TURN                      S10 S11 S12   ← hope re-enters, light returns
                                   /
AWE                            S13 S14 S15   ← release
                                         /
REUNION                              S16 S17
                                            \
REST                                         S18
```
Rule: **never resolve the grief before Beat 4.** The audience must sit in the ache of S05–S09 without rescue. The relief of S10 onward only works if we were brave enough to stay in the dark first.

## 6 · WARDROBE & COLOR CONTINUITY NOTES
- Same garment, two beats: Odessa's apron (S02↔S06) and Daniel's crewneck (S07↔S17) are deliberate costume echoes — the *thing* stays, the *person/light* changes. Keep them identical.
- No pure white, no pure black, no logos, no saturated primaries in Register A. Earth tones only.
- Gold enters wardrobe ONLY from Beat 5 (tree-light catching fabric) — never before, or the "turn" loses its warmth-reveal.
- Amara's beaded bracelet is a through-line prop: worn dancing with her father (S04), cradling the phone (S11). Keep it visible.

## 7 · 9:16 VERTICAL REFRAME MAP (for social)
- Faces (S07, S08, S09, S17) reframe cleanly — already centered.
- S05 (mugs): punch in on the withdrawing hand; lose the wide chair.
- S13/S15 (tree/forest): reframe to a vertical *rise* — trunk bottom to canopy; the vertical format actually helps the "growing up" motion.
- Keep supers in the middle-third safe area for both crops.
